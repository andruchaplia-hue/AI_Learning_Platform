[FORMATTING RULES FOR SOCIAL MEDIA POST (LIFESTYLE & THOUGHTS OUT LOUD)]
- STRICT LENGTH LIMIT: Exactly 1 single short paragraph (strictly 2 to 4 sentences maximum, strictly under 70 words total).
- Style: Spontaneous, candid, thoughts-out-loud observation (like a natural personal Telegram, Threads, or Instagram note).
- STRICT PROHIBITIONS:
  * Do NOT write multiple paragraphs or long essays.
  * Do NOT use formal headings (no H1, H2, H3).
  * Do NOT use bullet points, numbered lists, or outlines.
  * Do NOT use corporate speak, boilerplate marketing phrases, or AI clichés.
- Tone: Natural human voice, genuine reaction, relatable perspective.
- Emojis & Tags: 1-2 natural emojis. 1-2 optional subtle hashtags at the very bottom.
