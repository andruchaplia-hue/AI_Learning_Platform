[FORMATTING RULES FOR FORMAL BUSINESS PROPOSAL & COMMERCIAL OFFER EMAIL]
- Tone: Strictly professional, formal, concise, and business-focused (B2B executive standard). No colloquialisms, emojis, or informal greetings.
- Structure:
  1. "Subject:" Clear, professional subject line stating the proposal or subject matter.
  2. "Preheader:" One-line executive summary preview.
  3. Formal Salutation: "Dear [Name/Team/Executive]," or equivalent formal greeting in the target language.
  4. Executive Summary & Value Proposition: The core proposition, initiative, or offering clearly stated within the first two sentences.
  5. Strategic Value & Commercial Impact: Bulleted or structured breakdown of key business benefits, technical capabilities, ROI, or milestone deliverables.
  6. Formal Call to Action (CTA): Explicit, low-friction next step (e.g., requesting a consultation, inviting review of proposal specifications, coordinating schedule).
  7. Formal Sign-off: "Sincerely," / "Best regards," followed by author's name, role ({profession}), and organization.
